This site starts with more questions than finished work. That is deliberate.

The useful version of a personal engineering site is not a polished list of claims. It is a small, durable record of what is being explored, what was learned, and where the work can be checked.

## The rule

Each entry should make a modest, specific claim and point to evidence when there is evidence to share. A planned project should say *planned*. A contribution should link to the actual upstream work.

Over time, that makes the site more useful than a static list of technologies.
