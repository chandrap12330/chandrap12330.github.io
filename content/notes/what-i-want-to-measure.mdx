A local voice experience is not defined by a single model benchmark. It is defined by the time between a person speaking and the system responding in a way that feels natural.

## Initial measurements

- Speech-end to transcription availability
- Time until the first generated text token
- Time until the first generated audio sample
- Interruption and cancellation response time

These are questions to investigate, not results. As experiments are run, this note can grow into a documented benchmark methodology.
