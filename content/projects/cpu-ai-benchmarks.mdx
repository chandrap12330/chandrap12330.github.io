## Intent

This planned project will document repeatable measurements for AI workloads on CPU-oriented hardware. Benchmarking is only useful when the workload, machine, model settings, and measurement method are clear.

## What it may cover

- Speech-to-text latency and throughput
- Time-to-first-token and generation speed for local language models
- Text-to-speech startup and streaming latency

## Status

No benchmark claims are being made yet. Results and methodology will be published together once testing begins.
